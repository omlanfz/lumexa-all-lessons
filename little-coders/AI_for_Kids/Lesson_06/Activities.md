# Activities — Lesson 6: Voice-Activated Story

## Activity 1: Play Both Paths (15 min)
1. Play through the whole story once, choosing option 1 at the branch point.
2. Play again, choosing option 2. Compare the two endings.

## Activity 2: Map the Broadcasts (15 min)
1. Open the code for each scene/sprite.
2. List every broadcast name you find and which scene it leads to.
3. Draw a simple story map with boxes and arrows.

## Activity 3: Find the Voice Stand-In (10 min)
1. Find exactly which key press or click currently represents a spoken command.
2. Write down what real word it should eventually represent (check Scratch_Integration_Guide.md).

## Activity 4: Add a Line (10 min)
Write one new sentence of dialogue you would add to your favorite scene.
