# Student Guide — Lesson 6: Voice-Activated Story

Today you explore an interactive story that YOU help control! 🚀

## What's inside
- A story with multiple scenes.
- At least one choice point where you press a number key or click to decide what happens next.
- Broadcasts that move the story from scene to scene (just like Course 11!).

## Try it
1. Open `project.sb3`, click the green flag.
2. Read the story and make your choice when asked.
3. Play again and choose differently — see the other path!

## Look at the code
Find the broadcast names used to move between scenes. Are they spelled exactly the same everywhere they're used?

## Important truth
🤖 Robot Tip: Right now, number keys/clicks stand in for spoken voice commands. Later, following the `AI_Training_Guide.md`, you can train a REAL voice model in ML4Kids and swap it in!

## New words
- **Branching story:** a story where the reader's choices lead to different paths and endings.
- **Scene:** one part of the story, often its own backdrop and dialogue.
