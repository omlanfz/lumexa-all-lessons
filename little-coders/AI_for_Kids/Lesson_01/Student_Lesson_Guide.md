# Student Guide — Lesson 1: What Is AI?

Hi coder! 🚀 Today we are NOT writing code. Instead, we are learning how computers can **learn** — kind of like you do!

## What you'll learn
- What "AI" means (Artificial Intelligence)
- How a computer learns from examples instead of rules
- The 5 steps: **Examples → Training → Model → Prediction → Action**

## The Big Idea
You already know a cat from a dog because you've SEEN lots of cats and dogs before. Nobody gave you a rule book. AI computers learn the exact same way — by looking at LOTS of examples until they spot patterns.

## Try it yourself
1. Look at the picture cards your teacher gives you.
2. Sort them into two piles.
3. Say out loud: "I think this pile is ___ because ___."
4. Try to guess a brand-new card using your pattern — that's just like a computer making a **prediction**!

## New words you learned
- **AI (Artificial Intelligence):** a computer program that can learn from examples.
- **Training:** showing the computer lots of examples so it can find patterns.
- **Model:** the "pattern brain" the computer builds after training.
- **Prediction:** the computer's smart guess about something new.

🤖 Robot Tip: I'm not magic, I just study a LOT of examples!

## Remember from Course 10 & 11
You already taught the computer exact RULES with code (like "if you touch the edge, bounce"). Today you learned that AI teaches the computer using EXAMPLES instead of exact rules!
