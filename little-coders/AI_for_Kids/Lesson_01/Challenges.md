# Challenges — Lesson 1: What Is AI?

## 🎮 Mini Challenge: Two-Pile Sort
Sort 8 random classroom objects into two piles using your own secret rule. Can a partner guess your rule in under 3 tries?

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Think of THREE categories instead of two for your sorting rule. Explain why having more categories makes the sorting trickier for both humans and computers.

## 🏆 Fun Challenge: Explain It Like a Teacher
Pretend you are the teacher! In 3 simple sentences, explain to a stuffed animal or family member what AI is and how it learns, using the words "examples," "model," and "prediction."

## 🎨 Take-Home / Creative Challenge
At home, find 5 objects (in your room, kitchen, or backpack) and sort them into two groups using a rule you invent. Ask a family member to guess your rule. Then flip it — let them invent a rule and you guess theirs!
