# Activities — Lesson 1: What Is AI?

## Activity 1: Cat or Dog Sort (10 min)
Materials: `assets/` sorting cards (or hand-drawn shapes).
1. Spread out 10 mixed cards.
2. Sort into two piles: cats and dogs (or shape A and shape B).
3. Write one sentence describing the pattern you see in each pile.
4. Swap piles with a partner and check if their pattern matches yours.

## Activity 2: Guess My Rule (10 min)
1. One student secretly picks a rule (e.g., "all objects that are round").
2. They sort 6 classroom objects into two groups following the rule, without saying it.
3. Classmates study both groups and guess the hidden rule.
4. Reveal the answer and discuss how this is like a computer looking for patterns.

## Activity 3: My AI Notebook Page (10 min)
1. Draw or type three labeled boxes: **Examples**, **Model**, **Prediction**.
2. In "Examples," describe something you have seen a LOT of (e.g., 30 photos of your dog).
3. In "Model," describe the pattern you think you'd notice.
4. In "Prediction," describe what your brain (or a computer) could guess about a brand-new photo.

## Activity 4: Spot the AI (5 min)
As a group, list every AI-powered thing you can think of that you or your family uses (examples: face unlock, video recommendations, spam filters, voice assistants). Circle the one you think is the coolest.
