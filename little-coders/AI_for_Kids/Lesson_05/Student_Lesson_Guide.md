# Student Guide — Lesson 5: Training a Sound Recognizer

Today your computer learns to listen to YOUR voice! 🚀

## Steps
1. Go to **machinelearningforkids.co.uk** and open your class project. Choose recognition type **Sounds** (or open a Sounds project).
2. In the **Train** tab, add 2 labels (like "go" and "stop").
3. Click the microphone under each label and record yourself saying the word clearly, several times, until you have 15-20 examples per label.
4. Add a "background noise" label too — record a few seconds of quiet room sound.
5. Go to **Learn & Test**, train your model, then test it by speaking into the mic.

## New words
- **Sound wave:** the up-and-down pattern of a sound over time — this is what the AI studies.
- **Background noise label:** examples of "nothing being said," so the AI can tell silence from speech.

🤖 Robot Tip: Speak clearly and at a normal volume — mumbling or whispering confuses me!

🔍 Debug Detective: If I keep mixing up your words, check for background noise or too few examples.
