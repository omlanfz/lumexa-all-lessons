# Activities — Lesson 5: Training a Sound Recognizer

## Activity 1: Choose Your Words (5 min)
Pick two clearly different-sounding words with your partner (e.g., "go" and "stop").

## Activity 2: Record Examples (15 min)
1. Record 15-20 clear examples per word.
2. Add a "background noise" label with a few quiet clips.

## Activity 3: Train and Test (15 min)
1. Train your model in Learn & Test.
2. Test each word 5 times and record how many were correct.

## Activity 4: Fix Problems (10 min)
If accuracy is below 80%, add more/clearer examples for the weaker label, retrain, and test again.

## Activity 5: Reflect (5 min)
Write: "What made my sound model better — more examples, clearer speaking, or less background noise?"
