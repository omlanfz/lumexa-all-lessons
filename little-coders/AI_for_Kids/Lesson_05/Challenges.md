# Challenges — Lesson 5

## 🎮 Game Challenge
Get both spoken words recognized correctly 4 out of 5 times.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Add a third spoken command and test all three together.

## 🔍 Debug Detective
Purposely whisper your word or add background chatter — does accuracy drop? Explain why.

## 🎨 Take-Home / Creative Challenge
Think of a fun voice-controlled Scratch project you could build later (e.g., "sit" and "jump" for a dog character) and write down your two chosen commands.
