# Challenges — Lesson 3

## 🎮 Game Challenge
Make the sprite change costume AND play a sound whenever the AI recognizes a label.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Add a third label and a third if/else-if branch in your Scratch script.

## 🔍 Debug Detective
If your Scratch block never seems to recognize correctly, check: did you retrain after adding examples? Did you open Scratch through "Make" (not a blank project)?

## 🎨 Take-Home / Creative Challenge
Sketch (on paper or in a doc) an idea for a full AI Scratch game that uses this AI+Scratch bridge. What would the labels be? What would happen in the game for each label?
