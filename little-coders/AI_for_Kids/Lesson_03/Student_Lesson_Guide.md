# Student Guide — Lesson 3: Connecting AI to Scratch with ML4Kids

Today you build a BRIDGE between your trained AI model and a real Scratch game! 🚀

## Steps
1. Go to **machinelearningforkids.co.uk** and log in with your class login.
2. Click **+ Add new project**. Choose recognition type **Images**.
3. Open your project, go to the **Train** tab, and add 2 labels/buckets (like "Happy" and "Sad").
4. Add at least 20-25 example pictures per label using your webcam.
5. Go to **Learn & Test**, click **Train new machine learning model**, and wait.
6. Test it live with your webcam in the Test box.
7. Click **Make**, then **Scratch 3**. A special Scratch editor opens with a NEW block just for your model!
8. Build this script: When green flag clicked → forever → if (recognise image) = (Happy) then say "You look happy!" else say "Show me a happy face!"

## New words you learned
- **Custom block:** a special Scratch block made just for YOUR trained model.
- **Bucket/label:** one category of examples in ML4Kids (like "Happy").

🤖 Robot Tip: The AI block only appears if you open Scratch through the "Make" button — not a regular Scratch project!
