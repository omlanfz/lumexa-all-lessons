# Student Guide — Lesson 2: Training Your First Image Recognition Model

Today YOU are the teacher of a real computer brain! 🚀

## What you'll do
Use a free website called **Teachable Machine** to show your webcam pictures and train a real AI model.

## Step-by-step
1. Go to **teachablemachine.withgoogle.com**.
2. Click **Get Started**.
3. Click **Image Project** → **Standard image model**.
4. Rename "Class 1" and "Class 2" to two things you want the computer to tell apart (example: "Thumbs Up" and "Thumbs Down").
5. Click **Webcam** under each class and hold the record button while you show your example — try different angles and distances! Get at least 25-30 pictures per class.
6. Click the big **Train Model** button and wait.
7. Test it! Show your webcam your gesture and watch the confidence bars move.

## New words you learned
- **Confidence:** how sure the model is about its guess, shown as a percentage.
- **Class:** one of the categories the model is learning to tell apart.

🤖 Robot Tip: If I keep getting confused, give me MORE examples from different angles!

🔍 Debug Detective: Is your model always guessing wrong? Check if your two classes look too similar, or if the lighting is different between them!

## Remember
This model learned from YOUR examples — you didn't type any rules. That's the AI difference from Course 10 & 11 coding!
