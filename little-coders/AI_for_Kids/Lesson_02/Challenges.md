# Challenges — Lesson 2: Training Your First Image Recognition Model

## 🎮 Mini Challenge
Get both of your classes above 80% confidence when tested live 3 times each.

## 🔍 Debug Detective Challenge
Deliberately make a "bad" model with only 5 examples per class. Test it and see how often it's wrong. Then fix it by adding more examples. Compare the before/after confidence.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Add a third class called "Nothing" (just your normal background with no gesture) so your model can tell the difference between "doing something" and "doing nothing at all."

## 🏆 Victory Moment Challenge
Get a friend to test your trained model with a gesture you didn't personally record — does it still recognize it correctly?

## 🎨 Take-Home / Creative Challenge
Retrain your model at home with 10 new examples in a different room or lighting. Does the model become more confident everywhere, or only in one place? Why do you think that happens?
