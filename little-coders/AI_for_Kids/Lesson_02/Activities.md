# Activities — Lesson 2: Training Your First Image Recognition Model

## Activity 1: Set Up Your Project (5 min)
1. Open teachablemachine.withgoogle.com.
2. Click Get Started → Image Project → Standard image model.
3. Rename your two classes to something fun and safe (hand gestures work great).

## Activity 2: Collect Examples (15 min)
1. For each class, click Webcam and hold-record for about 25-30 seconds while varying your pose, angle, and distance from the camera.
2. Make sure the two classes are visually different from each other (not just lighting).

## Activity 3: Train and Test (10 min)
1. Click Train Model and wait for training to finish.
2. Use the live Preview panel to test each gesture 3 times.
3. Write down the confidence percentage each time.

## Activity 4: Improve Your Model (10 min)
1. Pick the class with the lowest confidence.
2. Add 10 more examples with different lighting/angle for that class.
3. Retrain and test again — did the confidence improve?

## Activity 5: Reflection (5 min)
Answer out loud or in writing: "What made my model more confident — more examples, or more varied examples? Why?"
