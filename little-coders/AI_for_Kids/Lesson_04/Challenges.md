# Challenges — Lesson 4

## 🎮 Game Challenge
Get 3 wins in a row against the computer!

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Add a "Best of 5" match counter that announces an overall winner.

## 🔍 Debug Detective
Find the exact block in the code where Rock vs Scissors is decided. Explain out loud why Rock wins.

## 🎨 Take-Home / Creative Challenge
Design (on paper) a NEW variation of rock-paper-scissors with a 4th choice (like "Lizard" or "Spock") and describe what beats what.
