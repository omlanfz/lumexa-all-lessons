# Student Guide — Lesson 4: Building an AI Rock-Paper-Scissors Game

Today you play and explore a REAL, complete rock-paper-scissors Scratch game! 🚀

## What's inside the game
- Press **1** for Rock, **2** for Paper, **3** for Scissors.
- The computer picks randomly.
- A referee sprite decides who wins and updates the Score.
- Win, lose, and tie all have their own sound and message!

## Try it
1. Open `project.sb3` in Scratch.
2. Click the green flag.
3. Press 1, 2, or 3 and watch what happens!
4. Play at least 5 rounds and watch your Score change.

## Look at the code
Open the code for the Referee sprite. Find:
- Where the computer picks its move randomly.
- Where your key press becomes "PlayerChoice".
- The if/else chain that decides Rock vs Paper vs Scissors.

## Important truth
🤖 Robot Tip: This game does NOT use your webcam yet — pressing number keys is a stand-in for a real hand gesture. Later, you can follow the `AI_Training_Guide.md` to train a REAL model and swap in real gesture recognition!

## New words
- **Stand-in:** something simple (like a key press) used temporarily instead of the real, final thing (a trained AI block).
