# Challenges — Lesson 7

## 🎮 Game Challenge
Get the Happiness variable to its maximum value.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Find out if Happiness changes on its own over time (not just from key presses) and explain why that matters for a "living" virtual pet.

## 🔍 Debug Detective
Press two mood keys quickly in a row — does the pet react correctly to the second one? Why or why not?

## 🎨 Take-Home / Creative Challenge
Design a whole new virtual pet character (name, appearance, and 3 moods) that could use this same Happiness-meter idea.
