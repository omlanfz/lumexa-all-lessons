# Activities — Lesson 7: Facial Expression Recognition and Virtual Pets

## Activity 1: Try All Three Moods (10 min)
1. Open project.sb3, click green flag.
2. Press H, S, and W. Write down what happens to Happiness each time.

## Activity 2: Trace the Code (15 min)
1. Open the pet sprite's scripts.
2. Find the `sensing_keypressed` block for H.
3. Follow it to the block that changes Happiness and the block that switches costume.

## Activity 3: Push Happiness to the Limit (10 min)
1. Try to make Happiness as high as possible.
2. Try to make it as low as possible.
3. Note anything special that happens at the extremes.

## Activity 4: Design a New Mood (15 min)
Sketch or describe a brand-new mood (e.g., "Sleepy") — what key would trigger it, what costume would it use, what would happen to Happiness?
