# Activities — Lesson 8: Showcase

## Activity 1: Choose Your Project (5 min)
Pick your favorite of the 3 portfolio projects to present.

## Activity 2: Practice Presentation (15 min)
1. Practice the 3-point structure (What it does / AI stand-in / AI pipeline) with a partner.
2. Get one piece of feedback and try again.

## Activity 3: Showcase (15 min)
Present your project live to the class or family, demoing it and explaining the AI stand-in clearly.

## Activity 4: Audience Questions (10 min)
Answer at least one question from a classmate or family member about your project.
