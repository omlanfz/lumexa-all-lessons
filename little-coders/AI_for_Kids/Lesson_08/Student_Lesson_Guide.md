# Student Guide — Lesson 8: Showcase Day!

Today YOU are the presenter! 🚀 Show off one of your 3 AI projects.

## Pick one project
- 🪨 Rock-Paper-Scissors Game
- 🗣️ Voice-Activated Story
- 🐾 Emotion-Recognition Virtual Pet

## Your 3-point presentation plan
1. **What it does:** Demo your project live — play a round, a scene, or a pet interaction.
2. **What stands in for AI right now:** Explain which key press or click is standing in for the real AI (like pressing 1 for Rock instead of showing a real rock hand gesture).
3. **The AI idea:** Explain Examples → Training → Model → Prediction → Action using your own project as the example.

## Practice first!
Practice your 1-minute presentation with a partner before presenting to the whole class.

## New words recap
- **Stand-in:** something simple used temporarily instead of the real, final AI feature.
- **Pipeline:** the order of steps AI follows: Examples → Training → Model → Prediction → Action.
