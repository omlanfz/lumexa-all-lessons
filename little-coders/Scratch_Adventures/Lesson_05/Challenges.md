# Lesson 5 Challenges

## 🎯 Mini Challenge
Change the repeat count in your square-walking script to 3, then to 6, and watch what shape appears each time. Can you guess the shape before you click it?

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Nest a small loop inside a bigger loop! Try: repeat 3 times, and inside THAT, repeat 4 times with a move + turn. Predict what pattern or shape will happen before you test it — then see if you were right.

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge
Look around your house for something that repeats a pattern — tiles on a floor, stripes on a shirt, a fence. Draw what you found, and think about how you'd describe it as a loop, like "repeat 6 times: one tile."
