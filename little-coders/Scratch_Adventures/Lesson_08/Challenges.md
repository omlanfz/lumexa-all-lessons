# Lesson 8 Challenges

## 🎯 Mini Challenge
Add a sound effect that plays specifically when the player wins your game, separate from any other sounds you've already added.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Add a second sprite (a friend character or an obstacle) AND a "Lives" variable with a losing condition (like "if Lives = 0, say Game Over and stop all"), so your game has both a win path and a lose path — just like a real video game!

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge
Show your finished game to a family member and watch them play it all the way through. Ask them: "What did you like best?" and "What's one thing I could add to make it even better?" Bring their feedback to share next time!
