# Lesson 8 Activities

## Activity 1: Game Plan Worksheet

Answer these on paper before coding:
1. What is my game's goal?
2. What controls does the player use (keys, clicks)?
3. What happens when the player wins?
4. What sprite(s), sounds, loops, conditionals, and variables will I need?

## Activity 2: Build the Score System

1. Create a variable named "Score."
2. Add "when green flag clicked" → "set Score to 0."
3. Add "when this sprite clicked" → "change Score by 1" → "play sound until done."
4. Test by clicking the green flag, then clicking your sprite a few times. Watch the Score go up!

## Activity 3: Build the Win Condition

1. Add a separate "when green flag clicked" → "forever" loop.
2. Inside it, add "if <Score = 5> then" containing "say [You win!] for 2 seconds" and "stop [all]."
3. Click the green flag and click your sprite 5 times to test your win condition.

## Activity 4: Build Your Full Game

1. Using your Game Plan Worksheet, build out your whole game: movement, sound, a loop, a conditional, and your Score variable.
2. Test after every few blocks by clicking the green flag.
3. Ask a partner to play your game and tell you what happened.

## Activity 5: Share Your Game

1. Prepare a 30-second demo: show your game's goal, controls, and win condition.
2. Present it to a partner, small group, or the whole class.
3. Ask for one piece of feedback: what did they enjoy, and what's one idea for improving it?
