# Lesson 2 Challenges

## 🎯 Mini Challenge
Add one more block of any color underneath your stack that you haven't used yet. Click it, watch what happens, and explain to a partner what you think that block does — just from watching it run.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Make your sprite do FOUR things in one stack: move, say something, change its costume (using a "next costume" block), and play a sound. Click the whole stack and enjoy the show!

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge
Sketch a brand-new sprite you'd love to bring to life next lesson — it could be a dragon, a soccer ball, your pet, or something totally made up! Write down one silly or fun thing you'd want it to say when it starts moving.
