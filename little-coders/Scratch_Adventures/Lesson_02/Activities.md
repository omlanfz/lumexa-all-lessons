# Lesson 2 Activities

## Activity 1: Meet the Cat Sprite

1. Open Scratch. The Cat sprite is already there waiting for you.
2. Find the blue **Motion** blocks in the block palette.
3. Drag a **"move 10 steps"** block into the empty code area.
4. Click the block by itself. Watch the cat move across the stage!
5. Click it a few more times — what happens?

## Activity 2: Make Your Sprite Talk

1. Find the purple **Looks** blocks.
2. Drag a **"say Hello! for 2 seconds"** block.
3. Snap it directly underneath your "move 10 steps" block (drag it close until you see a white line showing where it will connect).
4. Change the text inside the say block to something fun, like "I'm exploring Scratch!"
5. Click the top of your stack to run both blocks together.

## Activity 3: Add a Brand-New Sprite

1. Click the sprite button (a cat face with a plus sign) near the bottom of the screen.
2. Browse the sprite library and pick a character you like.
3. Click its name box and rename it to something fun.
4. Give it its own "move" and "say" blocks, just like you did with the cat.
5. Click the green flag above the stage — watch your project come alive!

## Activity 4: Explore a Mystery Block

1. Pick any block you haven't tried yet, from any color category.
2. Drag it under your stack.
3. Click the stack and watch closely — what did that new block do?
4. Tell a partner (or write it down) what you think that block's job is.
