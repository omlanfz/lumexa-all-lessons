# 🚀 Lesson 3: Motion, Looks, and Sound — Your Sprite's 3 Superpowers

## Hi, coder! 👋

Remember when you made your sprite move and talk for the first time? Today you're leveling up with THREE superpowers working together: **Motion**, **Looks**, and **Sound**.

## 🧠 Think Like a Coder: The Stage Map

The Scratch stage is like a giant map. Every spot has an address made of two numbers:
- **x** tells you left or right. Negative numbers go left, positive numbers go right.
- **y** tells you up or down. Negative numbers go down, positive numbers go up.
- The very middle of the stage is **x: 0, y: 0**.

The "go to x: __ y: __" block teleports your sprite straight to an address. The "move __ steps" block just walks it forward a little at a time, in the direction it's facing.

## Your 3 Superpowers

- 🔵 **Motion blocks** (blue) — your sprite's feet! Move, turn, or teleport.
- 🟣 **Looks blocks** (purple) — your sprite's face and clothes! Say things, switch costumes, get bigger or smaller.
- 🟪 **Sound blocks** (pink) — your sprite's voice! Play sound effects and music.

## Try This: Build a Sprite Show

1. Add a **"go to x: -100 y: 0"** block — this is your sprite's starting spot.
2. Add a **"move 50 steps"** block underneath.
3. Add a **"next costume"** block.
4. Add a **"play sound [pop] until done"** block.
5. Add a **"say [Wheee!] for 2 seconds"** block.
6. Click the whole stack and watch your sprite's mini show!

## 🧠 Think Like a Coder: Order Matters!

Code runs from TOP to BOTTOM, just like reading a book. Try swapping the order of two blocks in your stack — what changes? This is one of the biggest secrets of coding: **the order you put things in changes what happens.**

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge

Make your sprite look like it's really walking! Try: move a little bit, then switch costume, then move a little more, then switch costume again. Watch it "walk" across the stage.

## 🔍 Debug Detective

Uh-oh! Someone's sprite is supposed to get a little bigger every time it's clicked, but now it's HUGE — way bigger than the stage! What do you think went wrong? (Hint: try adding a "set size to 100" block to reset it back to normal before it grows again.)
