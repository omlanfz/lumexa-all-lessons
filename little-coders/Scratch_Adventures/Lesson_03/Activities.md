# Lesson 3 Activities

## Activity 1: Explore the Stage Map

1. Add a "go to x: 0 y: 0" block and click it. Your sprite goes to the center.
2. Try "go to x: -150 y: 0" — where does your sprite go?
3. Try "go to x: 150 y: 100" — where does your sprite go now?
4. With a partner, guess where a sprite would end up at "x: 0 y: -150" BEFORE clicking it, then check!

## Activity 2: Motion + Looks + Sound Combo

1. Start with a "go to x: -100 y: 0" block.
2. Add "move 50 steps."
3. Add "next costume."
4. Add "play sound until done" (pick any sound from the Sounds tab).
5. Add "say [Wheee!] for 2 seconds."
6. Click your whole stack and enjoy your sprite's little show!

## Activity 3: Order Matters Experiment

1. Take your stack from Activity 2.
2. Move the "say" block up to the very TOP of the stack instead of the bottom.
3. Click it and watch — what's different now?
4. Talk with a partner: why does moving one block change the whole show?

## Activity 4: Walking Animation

1. Add "move 10 steps."
2. Add "next costume."
3. Add "move 10 steps" again.
4. Add "next costume" again.
5. Click the stack a few times — does your sprite look like it's walking?
