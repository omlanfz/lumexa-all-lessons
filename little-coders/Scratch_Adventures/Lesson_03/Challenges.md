# Lesson 3 Challenges

## 🎯 Mini Challenge
Build a 4-block stack using at least one motion block, one looks block, and one sound block. Before you click it, guess out loud what will happen — then run it and see if you were right!

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Make your sprite "walk" by alternating move and next-costume blocks several times in a row, so it looks like real steps. Can you make it walk all the way across the stage?

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge
Without any computer, act out your own 3-part "show": one movement (like a jump), one face or costume change (like putting on a hat or making a silly face), and one sound effect using your voice. Perform it for someone at home!
