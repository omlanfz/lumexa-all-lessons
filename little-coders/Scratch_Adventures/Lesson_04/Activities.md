# Lesson 4 Activities

## Activity 1: React to a Key Press

1. Add a **"when [space] key pressed"** hat block.
2. Snap a **"move 20 steps"** block underneath.
3. Press the spacebar on your keyboard — does your sprite move?
4. Try changing the dropdown to a different key, like the up arrow. Test it!

## Activity 2: React to a Click

1. Add a **"when this sprite clicked"** hat block.
2. Snap a **"say [You clicked me!] for 2 seconds"** block underneath.
3. Click directly on your sprite (on the stage) and watch it react.

## Activity 3: Two Sprites, One Broadcast

1. On your first sprite, add: **"when green flag clicked"** → **"broadcast [wave hello]."**
2. Add a second sprite to your project if you don't have one already.
3. On the second sprite, add: **"when I receive [wave hello]"** → **"say [Hi there!] for 2 seconds."**
4. Click the green flag and watch both sprites work together!

## Activity 4: Broadcast Relay Chain (extra fun)

1. Add a third sprite.
2. On sprite 2, ADD to its script: after saying hi, add **"broadcast [scene 3]."**
3. On sprite 3, add: **"when I receive [scene 3]"** → any reaction you like (say something, change costume, play a sound).
4. Click the green flag and watch the message travel from sprite to sprite to sprite!
