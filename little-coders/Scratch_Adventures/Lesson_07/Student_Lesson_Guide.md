# 🚀 Lesson 7: Build Your Own Interactive Story

## Hi, coder! 👋

You've learned SO much: moving sprites, changing looks, playing sounds, reacting to events, using loops, and making decisions. Today, you're going to combine ALL of it to build your very own interactive story!

## 🧠 Think Like a Coder: Plan Before You Build

Every good story has a beginning, middle, and end. Before opening Scratch, sketch a quick storyboard — just 3 boxes:
1. **Beginning** — where the story starts.
2. **Middle** — something happens (a problem, a discovery, a surprise).
3. **End** — how it wraps up.

## 🤖 Robot Tip: Broadcasts Turn the Page

Remember broadcasting from Lesson 4? Today it becomes your secret story tool! A broadcast like "scene 2" can tell your whole project: "Turn the page — we're moving to the next part of the story!"

## Try This: Build Scene 1

1. On your main character: **"when green flag clicked"** → add 2–3 **"say [...] for 2 seconds"** blocks with your opening dialogue.
2. At the end, add **"broadcast [scene 2]."**

## Try This: Build Scene 2

1. On your Stage (or another sprite): **"when I receive [scene 2]"** → **"switch backdrop to [...]"** (or "switch costume to..." if using one sprite).
2. Add new dialogue for this scene.

## 🎮 Game Challenge: Add a Choice!

Somewhere in your story, add an interactive moment: **"if <key [space] pressed?> then / else"** — in the "then" branch, write what happens if the player presses space; in the "else" branch, write something different. Now YOUR story reacts to the player, just like a real game!

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge

Add a THIRD scene with another broadcast (like "scene 3") that wraps up your story with a fun, satisfying ending.

## 🔍 Debug Detective

Uh-oh! Someone built "broadcast [scene 2]" in Scene 1, but nothing happens in Scene 2 — total silence! What might be wrong? (Hint: does the sprite or stage that's SUPPOSED to react have a matching "when I receive [scene 2]" block? Did they pick it from the dropdown menu, or accidentally type a new name?)

## 🏆 Victory Moment

You just became a real interactive storyteller — an author AND a coder! Next time, in our final lesson, you'll build your very own complete game using everything you know. See you in Lesson 8! 🚀
