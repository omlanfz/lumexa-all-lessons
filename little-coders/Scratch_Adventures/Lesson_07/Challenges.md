# Lesson 7 Challenges

## 🎯 Mini Challenge
Add a third scene to your story using a new broadcast (like "scene 3") that gives your story a satisfying ending.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Add a second character sprite that also reacts to your scene broadcasts, so more than one character is talking and reacting during your story — just like a real cast of characters!

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge
Think of a surprising TWIST that could happen in a scene you haven't built yet — something unexpected that changes the story. Sketch it out on paper, and bring your idea to share next lesson!
