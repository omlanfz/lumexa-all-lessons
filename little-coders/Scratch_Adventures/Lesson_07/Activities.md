# Lesson 7 Activities

## Activity 1: Storyboard Planning

1. On paper, draw 3 boxes in a row: Beginning, Middle, End.
2. In each box, draw or write what happens in your story.
3. Give your main character a name and a simple goal (find something, solve a problem, go on an adventure).

## Activity 2: Code Scene 1

1. On your main character sprite, add "when green flag clicked."
2. Add 2–3 "say [...] for 2 seconds" blocks with your opening dialogue.
3. At the end of the stack, add "broadcast [scene 2]."
4. Click the green flag and check your opening plays correctly.

## Activity 3: Code Scene 2

1. On your Stage, or on a sprite, add "when I receive [scene 2]."
2. Add "switch backdrop to [...]" (or "switch costume to..." for a single sprite).
3. Add new dialogue for what happens in this part of the story.
4. Test the whole story so far with the green flag.

## Activity 4: Add a Player Choice

1. Somewhere in your story, add "if <key [space] pressed?> then / else."
2. In the "then" branch, write dialogue for what happens if the player presses space.
3. In the "else" branch, write dialogue for what happens if they don't.
4. Test both outcomes by pressing (and not pressing) space at that moment.

## Activity 5: Wrap It Up

1. Add a "broadcast [scene 3]" at the end of Scene 2.
2. On your Stage or a sprite, add "when I receive [scene 3]" with a final ending message.
3. Play your whole story from the beginning using the green flag!
