# Lesson 6 Challenges

## 🎯 Mini Challenge
Change the condition in your if/else script from "key space pressed?" to "touching mouse-pointer?" and test what happens when you move your mouse onto your sprite.

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Add a second condition to your bouncing sprite — like "if touching [another sprite] then" — so it reacts differently (says something special, changes costume, or plays a sound) when it touches a friend sprite instead of just the stage edge.

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge
Write down 3 "if/else" decisions from your own day, like "if it's bedtime, then brush my teeth, else keep playing." Come back ready to share one of your favorites with the class!
