# Lesson 6 Activities

## Activity 1: Bounce Off the Edge

1. Add "when green flag clicked" → "forever."
2. Inside forever: "move 10 steps," then "if <touching edge?> then" containing "turn right 180 degrees."
3. Click the green flag — watch your sprite bounce back and forth!

## Activity 2: If/Else Switch

1. Add "forever" with "if <key [space] pressed?> then / else" inside.
2. In the "then" part: "say [Jumping!] for 1 second."
3. In the "else" part: "say [Waiting...] for 1 second."
4. Click the green flag, then try pressing and releasing spacebar. Watch the message change back and forth!

## Activity 3: Change the Condition

1. Take your if/else script from Activity 2.
2. Swap the condition from "key space pressed?" to "touching mouse-pointer?"
3. Click the green flag and move your mouse near or onto the sprite — does the message change?

## Activity 4: Two Conditions Combo (extra fun)

1. Add a second sprite to your project.
2. On your main sprite, add another "if <touching [other sprite]?> then" inside your forever loop.
3. Inside it, add a "say" block and a "play sound" block.
4. Move your sprites so they touch — watch the special reaction happen!
