# Lesson 1 Activities

## Activity 1: Human Robot Game 🤖

**What you need:** A partner and some open space.

**Steps:**
1. Decide who is the Coder and who is the Robot.
2. Pick a target spot in the room — like a chair or a taped X on the floor.
3. The Coder gives ONE instruction at a time out loud (example: "Take 3 steps forward").
4. The Robot does EXACTLY what is said — no guessing, no extra moves.
5. Keep giving instructions, one at a time, until the Robot reaches the target.
6. Count how many instructions it took. Can you do it in 5 or fewer?
7. Switch roles and try again!

**Tip:** If the Robot does something silly or wrong, don't worry — that just means the instruction wasn't clear enough. Try saying it a different way!

## Activity 2: Recipe for a Robot 📝

**What you need:** Paper and a pencil (or crayons if you'd rather draw).

**Steps:**
1. Pick an everyday task you do all the time, like:
   - Brushing your teeth
   - Tying your shoe
   - Feeding a pet
   - Getting dressed for bed
2. Write down (or draw in order) every single step needed to do that task — pretend you're writing instructions for a robot with no imagination.
3. Make sure you don't skip any steps! Would your robot know to put toothpaste ON the toothbrush before brushing? Write that down too.
4. Read your steps to a partner or grown-up. Ask them to follow your instructions EXACTLY as written — even if it looks silly!
5. If something went wrong, figure out which step was missing or unclear, and fix your recipe.

## Activity 3: Code Detective Walk 🔍

**What you need:** Just your eyes and your brain!

**Steps:**
1. Look around your classroom or your home.
2. Try to spot 3 things that might be running on code (a smart light, a tablet, a video game, a digital clock).
3. Share what you found with a partner or the class.
4. Bonus: guess what "instructions" that thing might be following. (Example: "When the button is pressed, THEN the light turns on.")
