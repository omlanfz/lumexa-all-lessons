# Lesson 1 Challenges

## 🎯 Mini Challenge
With a partner, write 4 instructions for "how to draw a happy face," as if you're talking to a robot that has never seen a face before. Don't forget: circle for the head, two dots for eyes, a curved line for the smile — say exactly where each one goes!

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge
Write instructions for a fun, silly task — like "how to do the best superhero pose" or "how to do a victory dance" — using at least 6 exact steps. Ask a friend or family member to follow your instructions exactly, without guessing what you "really meant." Watch what happens!

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge
Tonight, ask a grown-up at home to be your robot for one small job — like setting the table or picking out pajamas. Give them instructions ONE step at a time, out loud, and make them follow exactly what you say (even if it's a little silly!). Tomorrow, come back and tell the class: what happened when you forgot a step?
