## ⭐ Bonus Challenge: Build a Rocket Out of Text

Real coders sometimes draw pictures using just letters and symbols — this is called "ASCII art"! Try typing this rocket, one `print()` line at a time:

```python
print("   /\\   ")
print("  /  \\  ")
print(" |    | ")
print(" |PYTHON|")
print("/______\\")
```

Can you add a `print()` line underneath for some launch smoke, like `print("  ^^^^  ")`?

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge: Introduce a Character

Think of your favorite book character, movie character, or video game character. Write three `print()` lines that introduce them, just like you introduced yourself in class. For example:

```python

print("He is Mario's brother.")
print("He can jump very high and is scared of ghosts!")
```

Bring your lines to our next class — we'd love to hear about your character! If you have a computer at home, try running it in Thonny or on Repl.it to make sure it works.
