## 🎮 Mini Challenge: Mix In a Hobby

Ask the player their favorite hobby and mix it into a sentence:

```python
hobby = input("What is your favorite hobby? ")
print(f"You love {hobby}! That's awesome.")
```

## ⭐ Bonus Challenge: Math Inside the Braces

Try doing math right inside the curly braces of an f-string:

```python
age = int(input("How old are you? "))
print(f"In 10 years you will be {age + 10} years old!")
print(f"In 100 years you will be {age + 100} years old!")
```

Can you add a line that shows how old they were 2 years ago? (Hint: subtraction works too — `age - 2`.)

## 🏠 Take-Home Challenge: Family Birthday Message

Pick a family member and write an f-string birthday message about them:

```python
name = "Grandma Rosa"
fun_fact = "makes the best pancakes"
print(f"Happy Birthday, {name}! Everyone knows you {fun_fact}.")
```
