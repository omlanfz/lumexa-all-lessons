# Portfolio Review Checklist

*(Reusable — used as the final QA pass in Course 16 Lesson 8, and referenced again in Course
18 Lesson 3.)*

- [ ] All 5 sections present (Hero, About, Skills, Projects, Contact)
- [ ] Bio is honest, specific, and matches the bio formula
- [ ] 2–3 case studies present, each with Problem/Approach/Result/Learned
- [ ] Brand (color palette + type pairing) applied consistently across the whole site
- [ ] Site is live at a real URL
- [ ] Tested on a phone-width screen and a laptop-width screen
- [ ] No typos or broken links
- [ ] Contact method is safe (school email or contact form — not a home address or personal phone number)
- [ ] Nothing on the page is fabricated or exaggerated
