# Course 17 — Final Assessment Rubric

| Criterion | Beginner (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service definition | Vague or copies someone else's service | Clear but not specific to student's skills | Specific, scoped, and grounded in real Course 16 skills |
| Pricing & scope | Missing or unclear | Present but boundaries unclear | Clear included/not-included boundary with a reasoned price |
| Age-rule understanding | Cannot explain platform age rules | Recalls rules but not the reasoning | Explains platform age rules AND why they exist |
| Proposal quality | Missing key sections | Present but generic | Matches discovery details, professional tone |
| Client Simulation completion | Incomplete | Complete but thin documentation | Fully complete with clear reasoning at each stage |
| Integrity | Suggests bypassing rules or exaggerates | No bypassing, but some exaggeration | No fabrication, no rule-bypassing, honest throughout |

**Scoring:** 6–9 Beginner · 10–14 Developing · 15–18 Proficient
