# Course 18 — Final Assessment Rubric

| Criterion | Beginner (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile/presence | Missing or generic | Present, some genericness | Specific, honest, matches the student's real direction |
| Content (5 pieces) | Fewer than 3, or generic | 3–4, some specificity | All 5, specific, honest, non-exaggerated |
| Networking | No real outreach attempted | Draft only, not sent/practiced | At least one real, safe outreach attempt with adult awareness |
| 90-day plan | Missing or unrealistic | Present but vague | Specific, realistic, checkable monthly goals |
| Age-rule handling | Ignores LinkedIn eligibility rule | Aware but inconsistent | Correctly applies LinkedIn 16+ rule; portfolio-first framed as equally credible |
| Integrity | Fabricated claims/testimonials | Mostly honest | Fully honest, no vanity-metric chasing |

**Scoring:** 6–9 Beginner · 10–14 Developing · 15–18 Proficient
